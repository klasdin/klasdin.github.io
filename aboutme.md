My name is Katherine S Lasdin and I am a third year PhD student at the University of Washington in the School of Aquatic and Fishery Sciences. 

I grew up in a small town north of Boston, Massachusetts and was always surrounded by water. On a family trip to Alaska in 2011, I fell in love with the marine environment, and never looked back. I absolutely adored seeing all the megafauna and new I wanted to persue work to protect the oceans and all the organisms that call it home. During my undergrad, I was fortunate to study at the University of Maine, [School of Marine Sciences](https://umaine.edu/marine/), complete a semester at the [Darling Marine Center](https://dmc.umaine.edu) and a semester at [James Cook University](https://www.jcu.edu.au).

After graduating from UMAINE, I joined the [Brander lab](https://branderlab.net) at Oregon State University as a master's student. I reserached the abundance of plastics in black rockfish off the Oregon coast. Please see my Publications tab to learn more about this project. 

In 2021, I joined the [Padilla-Gamiño lab](https://padilla-gaminolab.weebly.com) at the University of Washington, School of Aquatic and Fishery Sciences. My current resreach focuses on microplasics, plastic leachates and microbiome samples from Hawai'i and Tetiaroa, French Polynesia. I invite you to see my Projects page to learn more about my current research.
